Solara Bay is a sun-soaked 2D top-down open-world crime game: steal cars, outrun the Solara Bay police across a 3200×3200 city, and run four story jobs for fixer Marta Delgado — from a quiet pickup at the Old Pier to a timed dash to the Docks with the whole harbour force on your tail. Traffic, pedestrians, a five-star wanted level with an SBPD dispatch board (named units, distances, tactics) and an Air One helicopter from four stars, car theft, fist and pistol combat, a HUD with a mission card, weapon slots, speedometer and live circular minimap, and a save that remembers your money and your missions. $3,400 in story payouts.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Move with WASD, enter a car with E, punch with F, shoot with Space or a mouse click, pause with P.
